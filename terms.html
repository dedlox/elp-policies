Terms for ELP Automation

Effective September 6, 2026 · Applies to the ELP Automation app and its Content Posting API integration

In short: This is a single-developer personal automation tool, not a public product. It only ever acts on accounts the developer owns and has explicitly authorized, and it operates within TikTok's own platform terms and Content Posting API guidelines at all times.

01 Scope

These terms describe the operation of ELP Automation, a personal content pipeline that generates and publishes short-form video content to social accounts owned by its developer. This app is not distributed to, or used by, any party other than its developer.

02 Use of the TikTok Content Posting API

ELP Automation's TikTok integration is used solely to publish video content that the developer has personally generated and reviewed the pipeline for, to an account the developer owns. It complies with TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and Content Posting API guidelines, including:

  • Publishing only original or appropriately sourced content (public-domain and freely licensed reference material)
  • Never using the integration to spam, mass-follow, mass-message, or artificially inflate engagement
  • Respecting TikTok's community guidelines for all published content

03 Content sourcing

Video narration and factual claims are grounded in Wikipedia article text; images are sourced from Wikimedia Commons under their respective open licenses. The app does not reproduce copyrighted narrative or artwork from any fictional franchise it may reference — lore-focused videos summarize publicly available, third-party encyclopedic descriptions of those franchises, not their copyrighted material directly.

This is a personal, actively evolving project provided with no warranty of uptime, accuracy, or fitness for any particular purpose. It is not a commercial product and carries no service-level commitment to anyone.
