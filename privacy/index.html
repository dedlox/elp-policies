Privacy Policy

How ELP Automation handles data

Effective September 6, 2026 · Applies to the ELP Automation app and its Content Posting API integration

In short: ELP Automation is a single-developer personal project. It posts pre-generated videos to social accounts the developer owns and has explicitly connected. It does not collect data from any other person, does not sell or share data, and does not run ads or trackers.

01 What this app is

ELP Automation is a personal content-automation system that generates short-form videos — history and science explainers, and game/TV lore breakdowns — from public, freely licensed sources (Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons), narrated with local text-to-speech and captioned automatically. It publishes those videos to social accounts the developer personally owns and controls, including TikTok via the Content Posting API.

It is not offered as a service to the public. No third party creates an account, logs in, or provides data to this app.

02 Data the TikTok integration accesses

When the developer connects a TikTok account, the app requests only the scopes required to publish video content on that same account's behalf:

  • Basic profile information (to confirm which account is authorized)
  • Permission to upload video content to that account

The app does not read direct messages, does not access other users' data, does not scrape follower or following lists, and does not collect analytics on viewers of the published content beyond what TikTok's own creator tools already show the account owner.

03 Where data is stored

Access tokens issued by TikTok are stored locally on the developer's own machine, are never committed to source control, and are never transmitted to any server other than TikTok's own API. Generated video files, scripts, and source material used to build each video are also stored locally.

04 What this app does not do

  • Does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties
  • Does not run advertising, analytics trackers, or cookies on this policy page
  • Does not collect data from anyone other than the developer's own connected accounts

05 Revoking access

The connected TikTok account's owner can revoke this app's access at any time from TikTok's own account settings under connected apps. Once revoked, the app can no longer publish to that account, and any locally stored token for it is discarded on the next run.
